Jersey Devil Recompiled @VERSION@

Open the launcher and select your legally owned Jersey Devil USA disc (SCUS-94907).
This release includes native code for the five secondary executables and all
five renderer-bank variants identified in the original disc archives. Fallback
remains available for unresolved execution paths. See docs/AOT_OVERLAYS.md.

OpenBIOS and its MIT notice are included. Retail game and BIOS files are not.
Optional widescreen and frame interpolation are available through Mods.

Internal Resolution is enabled at 4x for fresh settings. Choose 1x–32x in Mods; hardware and GPU-memory limits may clamp larger choices. Your saved choices are retained.
